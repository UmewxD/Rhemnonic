1 . How to Draw " Open Vs. Closed " ( Odd Vs. Even States )

Think of this like a circuit gate or a C-shape Vs. an O-shape:
• Odd State ( Open Gap ): Leave a clean , obvious break in the outline of your glyph ( like the letter C or U ) . This visually signals that the number is " incomplete " or asymmetrical ( odd ) .
• Even State ( Closed Loop ): Connect the two loose ends with a crisp line or curve so the shape forms a completely sealed loop ( like the letter O or D ) .
• Example for your guide: Take your symbol for 3 ( odd, drawn with an open gap ). Show what happens when you add + 1 to make it 4 ( even ) - the gap physically snaps shut into a closed loop .






2 .  How to Draw " Modifier Dot / Tail " ( Carry-Over Past 11 )

In Base-12 ( Googolex ), counting hits 11 ( E ) and then rolls over to 12 ( 10 ) . Instead of always writing an extra zero, a carry-over tail lets a single digit show that it just rolled past a full group of 12:
• The Sharp Spike ( Tail ): Extend the final pen stroke of your symbol outward into a sharp , straight flick or thorn   ( like the tail on a Q ).
• The Modifier Dot: Place a bold, detached dot right next to the top-right corner of the glyph ( like a degree symbol ° or prime mark ' ).
• Example for your guide: Draw your normal 2 , then draw 2 with a sharp spike/tail next to it and label it 2 + 12       ( Carry-over = 14 ).
